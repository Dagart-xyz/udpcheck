1. What this is

UDPcheck is a non-commercial research service that measures whether UDP traffic passes between your network and our servers. By using the site, you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy.

2. How to treat the results

  • A result applies to the servers checked and to the moment of measurement. The browser check is short (a few small packets and replies of 600 and 1200 bytes); it is weaker than a node measurement and does not replace it.
  • The cause of packet loss may lie with the provider, with a transit network or on your side (router, antivirus, VPN client). The service does not claim that a particular provider is at fault, it shows what is visible from the servers checked.
  • The service is provided “as is”, with no guarantee of availability or accuracy; results are not a legal opinion or evidence in a dispute. The support request text is a template, check it before sending.
